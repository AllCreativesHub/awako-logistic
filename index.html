A few things to know before your next move.

How do I buy a car through Awako Trade?

Choose a car, review its details and ask to arrange a viewing. Confirm the inspection report, ownership documents, full price and handover terms before paying. This demo shows sample cars only; no purchase can be made here.

How do I list my exotic car on Awako Earn?

Start with “Put your car to work” and share the car’s make, model and availability. The proposed onboarding includes identity, ownership and vehicle checks before a listing is approved. The form on this page is a local demonstration.

Is my car insured during a rental?

Cover depends on the vehicle, booking and insurer’s policy. Before accepting a rental, confirm eligibility, covered uses, exclusions, excess and the claims process. This demo does not confirm coverage or issue insurance.

How are mechanics verified?

Awako Pro’s proposed checks include identity, relevant skills and a workshop review. Mechanics provide their details and supporting information for review. A submitted application is not automatic approval.

What fees will I pay?

Fees depend on the service and booking. The final price should show the service charge, any platform fees and what is included before you commit. No fee schedule is published in this demo. The earnings calculator shows gross income before deductions.

How do payments and owner payouts work?

Confirm the approved payment method, payment schedule, cancellation rules and payout timing in your booking agreement. This page does not take payments or process payouts. Never send money based solely on a sample listing.
